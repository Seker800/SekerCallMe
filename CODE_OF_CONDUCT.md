# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Let Agent Speak to be welcoming, constructive, and safe. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository and in public spaces when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

For ordinary disagreements, keep discussion focused on the work and involve a maintainer when needed. For sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's GitHub Security reporting flow. Do not include private personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this standard. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
